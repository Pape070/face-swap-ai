How it works & tips for a clean swap
  • Backend detects the face in your photo, expands the box into a full-head region (scalp, hair, jaw), color-matches it to each video frame in LAB space, and blends with seamless cloning + feathered head mask.
  • Best source: front-facing, neutral expression, chin-to-hairline visible, no sunglasses.
  • Best target: one person, facing camera, stable light. Side profiles may fall back to the original frame.
  • If InsightFace + inswapper_128.onnx is installed, the app auto-upgrades to neural swapping.
  • Only swap faces you have consent to use. Don't create misleading videos of real people.
